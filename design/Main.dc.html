Four steps. You stay in control at each one.

Nothing is generated automatically. Every output has its own button, so you only pay for what you ask for.

STEP 01
Upload or link
Audio or video up to 2 GB, or a YouTube URL. Pick a language hint so Urdu never comes back as Hindi.
STEP 02
Add slides or notes
Optional. Photos of slides, whiteboards or handwritten notes are read and kept alongside the transcript.
STEP 03
Review the transcript
Speakers, timestamps, SRT and VTT with real timing. Full previews, one-click downloads.
STEP 04
Generate what you need
Translation, MCQ quiz, point-wise notes or minutes of meeting, each from its own button.
Who said what
Speaker diarization labels each turn. Reliable for up to three speakers, useful for panels and interviews.
Subtitles that line up
Cues are built from word-level timestamps, not guesses. Export SRT or VTT for YouTube, VLC or Premiere.
Urdu, Arabic, English and 80 more
Code-switching handled. Urdu comes out in Urdu script, never Roman Urdu or Hindi, and translations keep the timing.
Quiz yourself
MCQs with hints and a reason for every option. Answer in the app, see your score, download the set.
Notes and minutes
Point-wise study notes for lectures. Agenda, decisions and action items with owners for meetings.
Slides and whiteboards
Upload photos of the material. Text is read out, the images are described, and you can ask questions about them.
